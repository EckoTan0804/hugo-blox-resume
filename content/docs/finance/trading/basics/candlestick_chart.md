## What is (Japanese) Candlestick Chart

![截屏2025-09-27 22.17.19](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.17.19.png)

Candlestick chart consists of red and green "candlesticks", or "candles" lined one after another.

- ⁠Each candle gives you detailed information about the price movement within a specified time interval. 
- Japanese candlesticks are the best way to visualize the ups and downs of a price, so that you can spot potential opportunities to BUY or SELL.⁠⁠ Let’s take a more detailed look.

## The candle

⁠Every candle consists of a **body** and one or two "**tails**" called shadows sticking out of it. 

![截屏2025-09-28 10.42.56](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2010.42.56.png)

- <span style="color:  #4c9390;"> Green</span> is bullish candle 🐮 (⬆️, opening price < closing price), <span style="color: #d65d48;">Red</span> is bearish candle 🐻 (⬇️, opening price > closing price)
- The **body** indicates the range between the open and close prices for a specific time frame 
- The **shadows** represent the highest and lowest price levels reached for the set interval.⁠

### Body size

The bigger the Candlestick body, the more it represents <u>momentum gain</u> as price traveled a greater distance during that time period.

![截屏2025-09-28 10.49.19](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2010.49.19.png)

The smaller the Candlestick body, the more it represents <u>momentum loss</u> as price traveled a shorter distance during that time period

![截屏2025-09-28 10.50.05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2010.50.05.png)

**Doji**: Very small candlestick body, or close to no body

![截屏2025-09-28 10.50.58](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2010.50.58.png)

- Represents **indecision** from buyers and sellers, because price swung up and swung down during that time, but then closed close to the same price as where it opened, which means buyers and sellers got nowhere and, again,shows indecision.

## Why are Candlesticks Important to Understand?

- Candlesticks tell a story and are a reflection of what buyers and sellers are doing (mainly the large institutions),
- The Candlestick pattern that forms at that particular moment in time tells you a lot about what is going on in terms of the buying and selling occurring in the market.
- Traders will look at candlestick patterns and the movement of candlesticks to make trading decisions, which is also known as <mark>**price action**</mark>.

## How to Identify Trend

### Uptrend

{{< spoiler text="Runs and pullbacks" >}}

- Run / impulse move
  ![截屏2025-09-28 12.42.58](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2012.42.58.png)

- Pullback / corrective move

  ![截屏2025-09-28 12.43.22](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2012.43.22.png)

{{< /spoiler >}}

A moving uptrend makes **higher highs and higher lows**, meaning there is **bullish** **upwards** momentum in the market so you want to use that to your advantage and take **long** trades.

![截屏2025-09-28 12.43.36](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2012.43.36.png)

{{% callout  note %}}

**Price action psychology behind uptrends**

There is **bullish** upwards momentum in the market that **the buyers are in control**. So you lean towards taking long entries to trade with the moving "upwards herd momentum".

{{% /callout %}}


### Trend change: Uptrend -> downtrend

**Lower low forms**: You have your moving uptrend through the higher highs and higher lows before a lower low (relative to the previous low in the uptrend) forms, signaling a trend change from an uptrend to a downtrend.

![截屏2025-09-28 12.50.57](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2012.50.57.png)

**Lower low and lower high**: You have your moving uptrend through the higher highs and higher lows before a lower low forms, signaling a trend change from an uptrend to a downtrend.

![截屏2025-09-28 12.52.16](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2012.52.16.png)

**Lower high forms first**: You have your uptrend through the higher highs and higher lows before you have a lower high that forms, signaling a trend change from an uptrend to a downtrend.

![截屏2025-09-28 12.54.58](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2012.54.58.png)

| **Certainty** | **Pattern**                            | **Implication**               | **Strategy**                                                 |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Low           | Lower High (before any lower low)      | Early warning only            | Tighten stop-loss, take partial profits, wait for confirmation |
| Medium        | Lower Low (break of higher lows)       | Uptrend structure broken      | Reduce/exit longs, aggressive traders may try small shorts   |
| High          | Lower Low → Lower High → New Lower Low | Full trend reversal confirmed | Exit longs completely, best entry for shorts                 |

{{< /spoiler >}}

A moving downtrend makes **lower highs and lower lows**, meaning there is **bearish** **downwards** momentum in the market so you want to use that to your advantage and take **short** trades.

**Price action psychology behind uptrends**

A moving downtrend shows bearish momentum and that the **sellers are in control**. So you lean towards taking short entries to trade with the moving "downwards herd momentum".

{{% /callout %}}

### Trend change: downtrend -> uptrend

**Higher high forms**: You have your moving downtrend to the lower highs and lower lows *before* a higher high forms

![截屏2025-09-28 22.11.12](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2022.11.12.png)

**Higher high and higher low**: You have your moving downtrend, the lower highs and lower lows before you have a higher high form followed by a higher low pullback before the full trend change breakout and higher high.

![截屏2025-09-28 22.12.42](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2022.12.42.png)

**Higher low forms first**: You have your moving downtrend through the lower highs and lower lows before you have a higher low that forms first, followed by a trend change breakout and higher high.

![截屏2025-09-28 22.14.10](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-28%2022.14.10.png)

| **Certainty** | **Pattern**                            | **Implication**                      | **Strategy**                                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Low           | Higher High (before higher low)        | Early warning, downtrend may weaken  | Wait, tighten stop-loss, maybe small position entry |
| Medium        | Higher Low → Higher High               | Trend shifting, partial confirmation | Start partial buy, cautiously build position        |
| High          | Higher High → Higher Low → Higher High | Full trend reversal confirmed        | Enter long / buy stock, trend confirmed             |

Price is making the same highs and same lows and moving in a sideways direction (directionless) -> neither buyers or sellers are in full control

You can trade in both directions, meaning take long & short trades with confidence.

## Chart Patterns

By looking at the patterns that candles create,you can often guess if a certain market is about to continue in its current direction or reverse it. ⁠⁠ 

Patterns that show that the market could change direction are called <mark>**reversal patterns**</mark>.⁠⁠ They could be ⁠⁠

- **<span style="color: #d65d48;">bearish⁠⁠</span>**: ones that indicate the change from an uptrend to a downtrend and should be perceived as a signal to SELL

   ⁠⁠![截屏2025-09-27 22.29.20](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.29.20.pngC)

- **<span style="color:  #4c9390;">bullish⁠⁠</span>**: these show that a price that has been decreasing is likely to start going up and we should BUY
  ![截屏2025-09-27 22.29.50](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.29.50.png)

### Patterns for Buy 🐮

#### Bullish engulfing

![截屏2025-09-27 22.31.39](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.31.39.png)

The bullish engulfing consists of a red candle, followed by a bigger green one, which *fully engulfs* the red candle.

![截屏2025-09-27 22.32.26](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.32.26.png)

The pattern is an indication of a market turning point - it is likely that the market may start going up ⬆️, and you can BUY. The indication is even stronger if the green candlestick engulfs two or three red candles.

#### Hammer

![截屏2025-09-27 22.36.13](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.36.13.png)

Pattern

- Short body
- Lower shadow: 2 - 3 times the length of the body
- Upper shadow: shorter or no shadow at all

The hammer is usually a clear indication of a declining price reversing its direction and starting to go up ⬆️, so you can start BUYing⁠⁠! 

But just to be safe, you could always wait for additional confirmation. Such confirmation is a green candle closing above the open of the hammer, for example:

![截屏2025-09-27 22.38.08](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.38.08.png)

#### Morning doji star

Appears when there is a slight market movement

![截屏2025-09-27 22.39.44](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.39.44.png)



It consists of three candles

- A red one
- Followed by a dojo

![截屏2025-09-27 22.40.07](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.40.07.png)

- Followed by a green candle closing beyond the middle of the first candle

   ![截屏2025-09-27 22.41.32](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.41.32.png)
   If the green candle is *longer* than the red one, the signal is even more reliable. 

### Patterns for Sell 🐻

#### Bearish engulfing

The opposite of bullish engulfing - A green candle is completely engulfed by a following red candle. 

![截屏2025-09-27 22.44.11](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.44.11.png)

When you spot it, you can SELL as the sellers on the market have possibly managed to overpower the buyers, and the price direction could reverse.

![截屏2025-09-27 22.45.14](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.45.14.png)

#### Dark cloud cover

The dark cloud cover appears *before* the price drops down. 

A long green candle is followed by a red one that opens at a new high and closes below the middle of the green candle.

![截屏2025-09-27 22.46.32](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.46.32.png)

When trading currencies, a second candle opening at a new high is rare, so the red candle usually opens at the close level of the green one. Once you see this pattern, you can go ahead and SELL, or you can wait for another, smaller red candle to form and confirm the dark cloud cover.

![截屏2025-09-27 22.47.46](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.47.46.png)

#### Shooting star

A red candlestick with a small body, a long upper shadow, and a short lower one.

![截屏2025-09-27 22.49.06](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EckoTan0804/upic-repo/master/uPic/%E6%88%AA%E5%B1%8F2025-09-27%2022.49.06.png)

When it appears at the end of an uptrend, it shows you that you can SELL, since the price, which has been rising up to that point, may start falling instead.
